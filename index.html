About MaraMarket

Built on campus,
for the whole community.

MaraMarket started with a simple frustration: students at Maasai Mara University had no safe, organised way to trade with each other. We built the platform we wished we had.

Our mission

Empowering students to trade safely, earn income and settle into campus life with ease.

Whether you're selling a textbook after your final exam, hiring a photographer for graduation, or searching for a bedsitter near Gate C — MaraMarket connects the community around one trusted platform.
